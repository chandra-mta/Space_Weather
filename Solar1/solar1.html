Realtime Solar-1 observations (source NOAA)
Plot interactions:
➤ Hover to display ACE p3: 115-193 keV.
➤ Single-click in the plot area to toggle ACE P3 trace visibility.
Single-click a legend label to show or hide its data trace.
Double-click a legend label to display only that trace.
Single-click again to restore a hidden trace.
Double-click again to restore all traces.
Click the "reset axes" icon (a "house" in the upper right corner) to revert to the original axes after a zoom.

The dashed black horizontal line coresponds to ACE P3 flux that results in a 2-hour fluence of 3.6 x 108 particles / (cm2 st MeV).
